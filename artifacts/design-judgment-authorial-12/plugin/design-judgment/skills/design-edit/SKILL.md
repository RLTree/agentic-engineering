# Design Edit

Judge the artifact before seeing deterministic defect findings. Return `ship`, `fix`, or `rebuild` and zero to three changes.

## Unanchored authorial pass

Inspect the compact brief, selected world card, first view, narrow view, and meaningful states.

Ask:

1. **Five seconds:** can a viewer say what this is, what matters now, and what happens next?
2. **Category swap:** could a neighboring product use the same structure and interaction after changing labels?
3. **Skeleton:** with copy, color, and imagery removed, does the composition still reveal the task and product mechanism?
4. **Mechanism to form:** where does unique product behavior affect space, interaction, feedback, progression, or evidence?
5. **Salience:** does the world card's primary element actually dominate while supporting and quiet content remain proportionate?
6. **Subtraction:** what can be removed, merged, or deferred without reducing success?
7. **World coherence:** do composition, typography, material, motion, copy, and states belong to one system?
8. **Earned signature:** is the memorable move grounded in the product rather than generic polish?

## Counterexample gate

Do not repair a pattern merely because it resembles a common AI habit. For every proposed change state:

- the exact observed harm;
- a valid context in which the current pattern would be correct;
- why that exception does not control here;
- the expected observable improvement.

Cards, serif type, translucency, dense data, conventional controls, and restrained motion are not defects by themselves.

## Evidence pass

Only after the authorial judgment is frozen, inspect the parent-provided truth-spine and objective evidence summary. Keep mechanical defects separate from ceiling judgment. Do not let detector output invent the design thesis.

## Output contract

- verdict: `ship`, `fix`, or `rebuild`;
- specificity verdict in one sentence;
- strongest protected choice;
- zero to three changes, each with exact region, evidence, expected effect, protected scope, and confidence;
- unresolved evidence limitations.

`ship` with zero changes is valid. A list longer than three means the critique has not prioritized.
