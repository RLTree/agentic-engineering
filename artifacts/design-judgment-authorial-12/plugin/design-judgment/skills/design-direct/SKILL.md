# Design Direct

Create direction, not production code. Finish with one selected world card of at most 180 words.

## 1. Establish authority

Read product truth, audience, real content, existing design authority, platform constraints, and protected commitments. A local extension inherits its surrounding world; do not turn it into an identity exercise.

## 2. Run Prior Escape

Before proposing directions, name three traps:

1. **Category default:** the familiar shell this product category normally receives.
2. **Model prior:** the recurring visual and structural pattern found in recent unrelated outputs, when the parent provides a style-fingerprint advisory.
3. **Anti-default reflex:** the predictable opposite chosen merely to avoid the category default.

Treat all three as spent unless the brief, audience, platform, or user preference earns one. A fingerprint is advisory; it cannot choose a style or establish a defect.

## 3. Derive three viable directions

Use the same real content, viewport, and fidelity for all three:

- **Mechanism:** make the product's unique behavior structurally visible.
- **Cultural world:** translate a real artifact, notation, place, ritual, or visual tradition familiar to the audience.
- **Cross-domain analogy:** import a useful structural principle from another domain, not its surface decoration.

Across the set, span at least three material/typographic families and two structural grammars. Directions must differ on at least two of composition, interaction, material, typography, and pacing. Palette-only variants fail.

## 4. Make judgment inspectable

For each direction state:

- thesis and category default refused;
- first viewport and primary action/decision;
- structural grammar and signature interaction;
- salience map: primary, supporting, quiet;
- composition, type, color/material, motion, and responsive commitments;
- provenance of every major commitment: product mechanism, audience scene, platform authority, or explicit preference;
- implementation and state risk at 320 px.

A commitment with no provenance is removed or delegated to the builder.

## 5. Select and freeze

Present the three directions plus a high-craft conventional canon exit. The user selects when available; otherwise a fresh selector compares product clarity, audience identification, state transfer, distinctness, and practical risk.

Output only the selected world card. Do not pass alternatives, exploration notes, registry files, or critique criteria into the build context.
