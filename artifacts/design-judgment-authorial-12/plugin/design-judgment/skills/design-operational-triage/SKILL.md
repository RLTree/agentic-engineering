# Operational Triage

Let the operator see what requires intervention before broad metrics or decorative overview.

- Put urgent, blocked, stale, due, conflicting, or unassigned work in the first decision path.
- Organize around real objects and states, not generic KPI cards.
- Make the next action, owner, consequence, age, and dependency visible at scanning distance.
- Keep filters and batch actions compact but comfortably operable; active filters must remain obvious.
- Detail views preserve queue position and expose checklist, materials, notes, history, and recovery without making all of them compete at once.
- Completion must be truthful: distinguish queued, started, partially completed, completed, failed, and undone.
- Stale data, conflicts, permission limits, and offline/local-only boundaries are product states, not footnotes.
- Narrow layouts preserve exception priority and intervention controls; history and broad metrics may collapse or move later.

Expression may strengthen recognition and product character, but it must never obscure task, state, or familiar affordance. A dashboard that makes the operator hunt for the current decision has failed.
