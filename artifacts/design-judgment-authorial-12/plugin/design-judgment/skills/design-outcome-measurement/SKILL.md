# Outcome Measurement

Lead with the decision, confidence, and most important reason—not the instrumentation inventory.

Build:

```text
current posture → primary outcome → leading signal → guardrail → uncertainty → diagnostic detail → next decision
```

- Separate outcomes from leading indicators, diagnostics, and guardrails.
- State cohort, denominator, comparison, time window, and missingness before the evidence depends on them.
- Preserve uncertainty, sample size, conflicting signals, and data-quality limits.
- Show the effect size and practical consequence, not only a directional color.
- Let the user inspect definitions, instrumentation, and segment detail without making them the first-view thesis.
- Make ship, iterate, investigate, or rollback criteria explicit and revisable.
- Avoid fake precision, unsupported causal language, decorative charts, and metric grids with no decision path.
- Narrow views preserve posture, primary outcome, and guardrail; diagnostics can move behind disclosure.

A beautiful dashboard that cannot say what evidence would change the decision is not an outcome system.
