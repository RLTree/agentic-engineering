## Authorial 12 flow

### Direction-open work

1. Parent compiles the compact creator brief.
2. Parent runs one explicit `dj-registry query` for `phase=direct`; valid abstention is allowed.
3. Fresh `design-direct` context produces three equal-fidelity directions and a canon exit.
4. Fresh Sol-High selector or the user selects one direction.
5. Parent validates the world card and discards the exploration transcript from downstream context.
6. Parent compiles the Truth Spine from the task, acceptance contract, fixtures, and selected direction.
7. Fresh builder receives only brief, world card, Truth Spine, fixtures, and zero or one specialist.

### Established-world or bounded work

Skip `design-direct`. Preserve existing authority. Parent still compiles the Truth Spine when mutable values, required states, effects, or narrow-width behavior matter.

### Editing and finish

1. Parent renders the stable initial artifact and freezes its bytes and evidence.
2. Fresh `design-edit` context sees brief, world card, renders, and meaningful states. It performs authorial judgment before the parent supplies the objective evidence summary.
3. Apply zero to three accepted edit changes.
4. Fresh `design-finish` context may apply bounded felt-craft changes only after structure is accepted.
5. Parent rerenders after every byte change.
6. Neutral repair may address a validated floor defect under the same budget for every condition.

## Style fingerprint

The parent records one compact fingerprint after each frozen initial artifact. It compares only against recent unrelated artifacts. Similarity is exempt when the artifacts share explicit brand/design authority.

An advisory flag requires the direction stage to reconsider at least two axes, but it cannot choose a style or independently block a candidate. Preserve both the pre-flag and post-response direction records for analysis. Do not send the fingerprint history to the builder.
