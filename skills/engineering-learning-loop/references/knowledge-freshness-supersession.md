# Knowledge Freshness, Supersession, and Retirement

Durable knowledge needs lifecycle control.

Every adopted learning should have:

- stable ID and version;
- provenance and evidence digest;
- applicable operating envelope;
- owner and review date;
- invalidation triggers;
- relation to prior rules: confirms, narrows, supersedes, or conflicts;
- deletion or archival path;
- generated human-readable projection when useful.

Search current authority before creating a new entry. Prefer updating or superseding one existing record to creating parallel guidance. Expired or contradicted guidance must stop appearing in default context even when retained for audit.

Evidence basis: R03, R129, R143.
