# Release Confidence

## Result

**Calibrated release confidence: 97.5/100** that Agentic Engineering 3.0 will positively influence the targeted architecture, lifecycle, verification, learning, Product Fitness, coordination, security, and Rust-engineering decisions when appropriately invoked in Codex, relative to underspecified work without equivalent controls.

This is a calibrated engineering judgment, **not** a measured probability that every task, repository, user, or product improves.

## Why the threshold is justified

The release changes several independent predictors of decision and delivery quality rather than relying on one larger prompt:

- single-front-door skill routing with 4,120 characters of conservative discovery headroom;
- first-class `no_change`, `partial_change`, `change_required`, and `blocked` dispositions;
- proportional verification selected from the actual risk, oracle, and truth surface;
- repair attempts treated as bounded information-gaining experiments;
- typed worker ownership, permission, budget, cancellation, evidence, and reviewer contracts;
- skill behavior evaluation with negative, overlap, hidden, mutation, and specification-evolution cases;
- governed observation-to-change learning with counterevidence, expiry, supersession, and retirement;
- Product Fitness evidence joining representative value, intervention, recovery, near misses, supervisory work, and continuance;
- full-lifecycle product and systems engineering from discovery through retirement;
- continuous identity, authorization, monitoring, update, containment, and recovery;
- typed Rust architecture, runtime, durability, protocol, observability, repair, and learning assets.

The mechanisms are encoded as decision procedures, templates, closed schemas, Rust domain sketches, evaluation cases, and release validators. They are not left as aspirational prose.

## Residual uncertainty

- No blind live GPT-5.6 Sol baseline-versus-v3 benchmark was executable in this environment.
- Rust reference modules were not compiled because Cargo and rustc were unavailable.
- No real product was enrolled in the Product Fitness or controlled-field pilot.
- Schema-generated examples establish structural satisfiability, not real interoperability.
- Skill activation and model behavior remain stochastic.
- A plugin cannot manufacture representative access, trustworthy outcome labels, causal identification, domain authority, or organizational follow-through.
- High-consequence products still require appropriate legal, safety, privacy, security, accessibility, and domain review.

## Release interpretation

The score supports release because every mandatory deterministic gate passes, the mechanisms are falsifiable, and unavailable empirical evidence is explicitly represented as unexecuted. A failed live A/B, adverse controlled-field result, excessive intervention burden, security regression, or repeated incorrect routing should lower confidence and may require narrowing or rollback regardless of this score.
