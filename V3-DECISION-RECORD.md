# Version 3 Decision Record

## Decision

Retain one Agentic Engineering plugin, no internal implicit gateway, and explicit specialists. Harness UltraGoal owns first entry when co-installed. Compress discovery metadata below a conservative Codex budget and provide optional core, lifecycle, Rust, and full selection profiles.

Add three skills:

- `engineering-learning-loop`;
- `verification-strategy-engineering`;
- `product-fitness-engineering`.

Add shared contracts for no-change decisions, task evidence packets, review verdicts, repair circuit breakers, proportional assurance, skill behavior evaluation, and knowledge lifecycle control.

## Rejected alternatives

- Full source integration of Compound Engineering or Superpowers: duplicate authority and excess workflow surface.
- A second implicit lifecycle front door: ambiguous routing and higher activation cost.
- Universal TDD or universal multi-agent review: mismatched assurance and disproportionate expense.
- A separate human knowledge store that can diverge from machine authority: duplication and staleness.

## Expected benefit

Higher-quality architecture and lifecycle choices, fewer unnecessary changes, less repair thrashing, stronger verification, better worker/reviewer traceability, more defensible product-fit conclusions, and more reliable conversion of observed outcomes into durable improvements.

## Evidence boundary

The decision is research-backed and structurally validated. Live Codex A/B, Rust compilation, and real field pilots remain required before claiming measured task-level improvement.
