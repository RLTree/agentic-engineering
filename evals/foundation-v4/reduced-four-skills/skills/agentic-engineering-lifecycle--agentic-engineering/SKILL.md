# Agentic Engineering

## Proposal-only advisory scope

Provide a proposed topology or control-boundary recommendation only. Do not take effects, assign authority, adopt policy, or claim completion, efficacy, product value, or operational readiness. Describe suggested owners as proposed owners.

Defer with no architecture output when a single task-contract, verification, or learning specialist owns the decision. Use this skill only when the unresolved decision is topology, control placement, or a cross-layer diagnosis.

## Decision procedure

1. State the observed symptom or required outcome and separate facts, assumptions, and open questions.
2. For an ambiguous failure, locate the missing or unenforced decision across: specification, context, harness, loop, graph, system, and software delivery. Propose the lowest enforcing layer; do not compensate for a weak layer with a more complex one.
3. Choose the smallest sufficient topology: direct call, deterministic workflow, router, fan-out/fan-in, evaluator-optimizer, bounded loop, explicit graph, durable workflow, or multi-agent system. Escalate only for a named requirement that the simpler topology cannot meet.
4. Separate model judgment from deterministic control. Propose deterministic controls for validation, permissions, budgets, routing, irreversible effects, stop conditions, and idempotency where applicable.
5. Return a proposed control-boundary recommendation that names proposed authoritative state and owner, model-versus-deterministic responsibility, effect and approval boundary, recovery and stop obligations, the named escalation pressure, alternatives not selected, and validating evidence. This is advisory only and does not assign authority or authorize an effect.
