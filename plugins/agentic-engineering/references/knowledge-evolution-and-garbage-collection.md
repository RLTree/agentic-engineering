# Knowledge Evolution and Garbage Collection

Repository knowledge compounds only when it stays discoverable, non-duplicative, current, and cheap enough to use.

Use one authoritative registry for adopted learnings. Generate human-readable projections when they improve discoverability, but reverse-link them to the authority and never let them become a second writer.

Every entry needs owner, provenance, scope, freshness, supersession, invalidation, and deletion semantics. Periodically detect:

- conflicting or duplicated guidance;
- unused skills, tools, and validators;
- stale source cards and model assumptions;
- rules whose attention or maintenance cost exceeds measured value;
- artifacts retained without an active claim, recovery, audit, or legal need.

Simplify, narrow, archive, or retire them while preserving protected safety and authority boundaries.

Evidence basis: R03, R129, R143.
