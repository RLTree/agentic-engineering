# Product Journey Review Contract

Map and exercise the complete representative journey rather than a list of pages or commands.

For each journey record:

- user/operator and job;
- starting repository, data, permissions, and dirty state;
- public entry surface;
- first value event and verified outcome;
- success, validation, permission, empty, failure, interruption, diagnosis, recovery, and repeat-use branches;
- external effects and preserved unrelated state;
- intervention and review burden;
- evidence pointers bound to exact identities;
- product, release, and continuance claim ceilings.

Use real representative work when safe. Shadow, recommendation-only, or approval-gated modes are legitimate steps when direct autonomy is not yet justified.

Evidence basis: R129, R131, R141.
