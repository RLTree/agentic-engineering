# Learning Adoption Record

A learning record is a decision object, not a retrospective narrative.

Required separation:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observation | What was directly recorded, with exact provenance |
| Interpretation | What the evidence plausibly means |
| Mechanism hypothesis | Why the outcome occurred and which control could change it |
| Counterevidence | Cases or facts that weaken the hypothesis |
| Intervention | The smallest proposed change |
| Evaluation | How the intervention could be falsified |
| Decision | Adopt, hold, narrow, reject, supersede, or retire |

A record is adoption-ready only when it names the authority that can own the change, the operating envelope, held-out evaluation, residual risk, freshness policy, and rollback. Keep one learning per record so overlap and supersession remain tractable.

The NIST continuous-monitor communication is emerging research rather than a
normative adoption requirement (`F-NIST-AGENT-INIT`). Historical lineage: R127,
R137, R138, R140, R148.
