# Product Fitness Observation

Product Fitness asks whether intended users achieve intended outcomes in the intended context with acceptable effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction, freedom from risk, recovery burden, and continuance.

The minimal observation row should contain:

- user/operator evidence class;
- representative work and operating envelope;
- first value and verified outcome;
- time to verified value;
- human interventions, review rounds, and correction effort;
- failure, diagnosis, recovery, and preserved state;
- accessibility, cognitive load, trust, and risk observations;
- exact source/package/install/runtime/journey identities or explicit withheld states;
- claim ceiling.

Do not build telemetry solely to populate the row. Use existing signals, direct observation, and a small manual-first ledger until recurring decisions justify automation.

Evidence basis: R131, R144, R145.
